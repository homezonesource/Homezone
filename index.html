About Us

Furnishing homes,
not just selling furniture.

Home Zone is a Coimbatore-based furniture and interiors group that brings several respected brands together under one roof — supported by in-house manufacturing and a showroom-first approach to buying.

Who we are

A group with range and roots.

Most furniture stores stretch one name across everything. We took a different path — assembling a portfolio of focused brands, each strong in what it does, so you get genuine choice instead of compromise.

From contemporary imported design to motion recliners, modular living to home décor, every brand we carry earns its place. And because we manufacture in-house, we can finish, customise and service what we sell.

What we stand for

Four things we don't compromise on.

01

Quality you can sit on

Every piece is judged by how it feels and how it lasts — not just how it photographs. In-house finishing keeps the standard consistent.

02

Honest choice

Distinct brands across styles and budgets, so the recommendation that's right for your home wins — never the one that's easiest to sell.

03

Made for Indian homes

Sizing, materials and finishes chosen for how families here actually live, with the climate and spaces in mind.

04

Service after the sale

Delivery, installation and support handled by people who know the product, because furniture is a long-term relationship.

Manufacturing & Custom

Made by us,
built to your spec.

Beyond retail, Home Zone runs its own manufacturing unit in Coimbatore — crafting sofas, recliners and upholstered beds, fully customised to each customer and to your architect's drawings.

What we make

Custom upholstery, crafted in-house.

Custom sofas

Made-to-order upholstery in your choice of size, configuration, fabric or leather, fill and firmness — from compact two-seaters to large L-shaped and modular sets.

Recliners & motion

Single-seaters, home-theatre layouts and reclining sofa sets — built for comfort and finished to match the rest of your room.

Upholstered beds & cots

Upholstered cots and bed frames with padded headboards, tailored to your bedroom dimensions and finished in your chosen material.

Bespoke projects

Built to the architect's drawing.

Working with an interior designer or architect? Share the drawings and we'll build to the exact specification — so the furniture fits the space as it was designed, not the other way around.

You control the details: overall dimensions and configuration, frame, fabric or leather, foam fill and firmness, leg style and finish. We translate it into a precise build and craft it in our own unit.

Discuss a custom piece
How it works

From brief to living room.

01

Consult & measure

We visit your space or work from your measurements and your architect's drawings to understand exactly what's needed.

02

Design & quote

We turn the brief into a build plan — configuration, materials and a clear, transparent quote before anything is made.

03

Select materials

Choose fabric or leather, foam fill, firmness, frame and finish from our library, with guidance from our team.

04

Craft & install

Built and quality-checked in our Coimbatore unit, then delivered and installed in your home.
